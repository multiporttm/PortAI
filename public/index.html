How PortAI protects your privacy
  • No ads, no tracking. No analytics, cookies, fingerprinting or third-party scripts — enforced by a strict Content-Security-Policy.
  • Encrypted history. Chats are encrypted with AES-256-GCM in your browser before being stored, and never leave your device.
  • No server logs. The PortAI server only relays your message to the model and streams the answer back. It stores nothing and never logs content or IP addresses.
  • Minimal forwarding. Only the conversation text and model settings are sent to the model provider — not your IP, cookies or browser details.
  • Open models, zero retention. PortAI only uses open-source models, hosted by Cloudflare under a zero-data-retention policy: prompts and replies are not stored or used for training.
  • Ghost mode. Chats that are never written to storage at all.
